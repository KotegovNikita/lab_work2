\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
An attack card that targets and damages a single enemy character. 

Inherits from \doxylink{classAttackCard}{Attack\+Card}. When used, this card strikes the first available enemy on the battlefield (typically used by AI), and may eliminate it instantly if its health drops to 0.
