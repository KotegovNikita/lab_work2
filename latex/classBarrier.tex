\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A support-\/type card that grants a shield (defensive buff) to an allied character. 

Inherits from \doxylink{classSupportCard}{Support\+Card}. When played, this card typically adds protection to a unit, though the specific logic is implemented in the overridden \doxylink{classBarrier_af952d092c4aef167ae1a2bf784a895d3}{use()} method.
