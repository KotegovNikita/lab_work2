\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A \doxylink{classWarrior}{Warrior} character card known for aggressive attack behavior. 

Inherits from \doxylink{classWarrior}{Warrior}. May have enhanced effects when summoned or while attacking, depending on the game logic implemented in the {\ttfamily \doxylink{classBerserk_a08c0d8e233774744dd56ff6ce2cb51a0}{use()}} method.
