\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Abstract base class for magic-\/type characters (e.\+g., \doxylink{classWarlock}{Warlock}, \doxylink{classElementalist}{Elementalist}). 

Inherits from \doxylink{classCharacterCard}{Character\+Card}. \doxylink{classMage}{Mage} can be used as a foundation for all characters that are spellcasters and may later be extended with unique mechanics.
