\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a Rogue-\/type character card with distinct properties or effects. 

Inherits from the \doxylink{classRogue}{Rogue} class. Can be extended with special behaviors in the overridden {\ttfamily \doxylink{classAssassin_a4b512a7ab07a00c932bcecab48d49f0c}{use()}} method.
