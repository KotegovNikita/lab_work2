\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A character card that represents a rogue who can steal cards from the opponent\textquotesingle{}s hand. 

Inherits from \doxylink{classRogue}{Rogue}. When summoned, the \doxylink{classThief}{Thief} attempts to randomly steal a non-\/\+Thief card from the opponent\textquotesingle{}s hand and add it to the player\textquotesingle{}s hand.
