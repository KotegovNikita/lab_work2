\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a strong melee-\/based character card. 

Inherits from \doxylink{classCharacterCard}{Character\+Card}. The \doxylink{classWarrior}{Warrior} class serves as a base for physical fighters like \doxylink{classKnight}{Knight} or \doxylink{classBerserk}{Berserk}, and can be extended with special traits such as rage, defense, or armor.
