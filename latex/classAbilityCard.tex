\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a card that applies an effect (e.\+g., damage, healing, buff). 

This is an abstract representation of any non-\/character card that directly influences the battlefield through a one-\/time effect.

Inherits from \doxylink{classCard}{Card}.
