\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A Mage-\/type character card with dark or aggressive traits. 

Inherits from \doxylink{classMage}{Mage}. \doxylink{classWarlock}{Warlock} is typically a summonable character with moderate attack and health, and may have special effects like health sacrifice or curses (extendable in {\ttfamily \doxylink{classWarlock_ac7520514cfa0ab320cef69ae997adea7}{use()}}).
