\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A character card with moderate stats, often extended by subclasses like \doxylink{classThief}{Thief} or \doxylink{classAssassin}{Assassin}. 

Inherits from \doxylink{classCharacterCard}{Character\+Card}. Rogues are typically fast, tactical units that may be used as a base for more complex subclasses with unique abilities (e.\+g., stealing, critical hits).
