Detailed Description

A character card that represents a rogue who can steal cards from the opponent's hand.

Inherits from Rogue. When summoned, the Thief attempts to randomly steal a non-Thief card from the opponent's hand and add it to the player's hand.

void Thief::use ( GameManager &  gm)
overridevirtual

Summons the Thief to the battlefield and attempts to steal a card.

Activates the Thief card.

If the opponent has any non-Thief cards in hand, one is randomly selected and transferred to the Thief’s controller.

Parameters
gmReference to the GameManager handling game state.

When used, the Thief is summoned to the battlefield with stats (1 ATK, 1 HP), and attempts to steal a random non-Thief card from the opponent’s hand. If only Thieves are in the hand, or the opponent has no cards, the ability fails silently.

gmReference to the current GameManager controlling the game state.
