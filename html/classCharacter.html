Detailed Description

Represents a combat unit summoned to the battlefield.

Each character has a name, attack value, health, and type. Characters can engage in combat, receive damage, be healed, and be destroyed. They are not playable cards, but rather active units placed on the board.

Performs an attack on another character.

This method applies this character’s attack to the target’s health.

Parameters

Applies class-based effects depending on the character type:

  • Berserk: Bonus damage when below 50% health.
  • Warlock: Sacrifices HP for extra damage.
  • Assassin: 30% chance to deal critical (double) damage.
